Professional Communication

A thoughtful approach to professional correspondence.

Clear communication is an important part of building professional relationships. This page contains an email template for enquiring about industrial attachment opportunities in business intelligence, data analytics, information technology, and related fields.

01 / Email Template

Industrial attachment enquiry

Use this template when contacting a company or organisation. Replace the bracketed information with the correct details before sending the email.

To [Recipient's professional email address]
Subject Enquiry Regarding Industrial Attachment Opportunities

Dear [Recipient's Name or Hiring Manager],

I hope this email finds you well.

My name is Thulani Dawin Seleke, and I am a first-year Business Intelligence and Data Analytics student at Botswana Accountancy College. I am writing to enquire about the possibility of undertaking an industrial attachment with your organisation.

I am interested in developing my practical understanding of data analysis, business intelligence, reporting, and the use of data to support informed decision-making. I am eager to learn from experienced professionals and contribute positively to the work of your team.

Through my academic work and previous administrative experience, I have developed an appreciation for organisation, communication, teamwork, and accurate information management. I would welcome the opportunity to build on these foundations in a professional environment.

I would appreciate it if you could advise me whether industrial attachment opportunities are available within your organisation and share any relevant application requirements.

I have attached my CV for your consideration and would be happy to provide any additional information required.

Thank you for your time and consideration. I look forward to hearing from you.

Kind regards,

02 / Before Sending

Make every email specific.

A professional enquiry should show that you understand whom you are contacting and why their organisation interests you.

  • Address the correct person whenever their name is available.
  • Use the organisation's actual name in your message.
  • State your expected attachment dates if they are confirmed.
  • Attach your CV and any other documents requested.
  • Use a professional email address and check your spelling.
  • Follow the organisation's application instructions.
Important: Do not send the template without reviewing it. Confirm that the organisation offers relevant opportunities, personalise the message, and attach your CV before sending.
