Learning to think with data

01

Looking beyond the numbers to understand what information can actually tell us.

Reflection · Data analytics

Numbers become useful when they answer a question.

Data analytics is more than producing charts or calculating totals. It begins with understanding a question, examining the information available and deciding how to communicate what the data supports.

Through practical work with datasets and dashboards, I am learning the importance of preparation, accuracy and choosing visualisations that make information easier to understand.

I am also learning to distinguish between what the data demonstrates and what still needs further investigation. A useful analysis should make information clearer without claiming more than the evidence supports.

A principle I am developing: understand first, visualise second, conclude carefully.
