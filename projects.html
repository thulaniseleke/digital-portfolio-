PROJECT 01

NYC Taxi Data Analysis

A hands-on analytics exercise using a New York City taxi dataset. The work involved preparing data, exploring quantitative and categorical variables, building charts, and developing an interactive dashboard in Power BI.

The project provided an opportunity to practise moving from raw information towards a structured, visual report.

Work undertaken

  • Cleaned and prepared dataset fields in Power Query.
  • Explored quantitative and categorical variables.
  • Created visualisations to organise and present data.
  • Worked with interactive slicers and KPI measures.
  • Used Excel alongside Power BI during the analysis workflow.

Measures explored

  • Average fare
  • Average tip amount
  • Average trip distance
  • Total revenue
  • Total trips

Working principles

How I approach a problem

Start with the information

Understand the available data, identify its structure and consider what can reasonably be learned from it before choosing charts or drawing conclusions.

Make the result understandable

Present information with clear labels, appropriate visualisations and useful summaries so that the result is easier to interpret.

Check the details

Pay attention to data quality, calculations and the relationship between the information presented and the question being explored.

Keep learning

Treat each practical exercise as an opportunity to strengthen technical knowledge, improve problem-solving and develop a more disciplined analytical process.
